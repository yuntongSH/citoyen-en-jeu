Public availability is not an open-source licence. Do not upload the official PDFs, question-bank wording, photographs, graphic identity or other third-party material without establishing the relevant reuse rights. The repository licence grants rights only to our own contributions. Facts, dates and short legal expressions are distinguished from the publisher’s authored explanations and layout.

Lesson page links are navigation aids, not proof that every sentence comes from that single page. Improving fact-level citations is an outstanding editorial task. Resolve disagreements using current primary sources and record the change; do not invent an official answer key.

Priority contributions: complete the missing curriculum, improve source references, separately map each exam, write stronger original practice questions, and review accessibility and translation. Do not present the introductory course as meeting every official exam objective.
